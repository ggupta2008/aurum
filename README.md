# Aurum: AI-First Wealth Intelligence Platform

> **Conversational CFP/CPA Advisor** | Institutional-Grade Analysis | Tax-Efficiency First | Privacy-Centric

Aurum is a premium, S-Tier wealth intelligence platform that transforms traditional financial planning into an **AI-driven conversational experience**. Powered by Google Gemini, Aurum acts as a world-class fiduciary advisor with dual CFP and CPA certifications, providing prescriptive, tax-optimized, and estate-aware guidance for high-net-worth individuals.

---

## 🎯 **Platform Vision**

Aurum moves beyond static calculators and complex forms. Our **AI-First** architecture puts a conversational fiduciary at the center of the experience, leveraging a deterministic financial engine to provide instant, accurate, and transparent wealth advice.

- ✅ **Everything is AI-Driven** - No hardcoded rules. The advisor analyzes your unique profile in real-time.
- ✅ **Fiduciary Standard** - Institutional-grade advice citing specific tax codes (IRC §72(t), §1014, §2010).
- ✅ **Deterministic Math** - AI insights are backed by a robust 25-year projection and Monte Carlo engine.
- ✅ **Privacy First** - All data remains in your local browser storage. No data is sold or stored on our servers.

## 🚀 **Key Features**

### **1. AI Wealth Advisor (Fiduciary Console)**
- **Conversational Interface** - Chat naturally about your goals, risks, and financial situation.
- **Context-Aware Analysis** - The AI "sees" your entire scoped profile, including granular tax lots and family structures.
- **Strategy Synchronization** - One-click deployment of AI-recommended protocols (Roth conversions, TLH, etc.) into the live financial engine.
- **IRC Citations** - Professional-grade reasoning with citations to the Internal Revenue Code.

### **2. Advanced Financial Engine**
- **25-Year Projections** - Side-by-side comparison of "Status Quo" vs. AI-optimized trajectories.
- **Monte Carlo Simulator** - Stress-tests your plan against 250+ market scenarios.
- **Tax Waterfall & Heatmap** - Visualizes tax impact and identifies optimal Roth conversion windows.
- **Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR)** - Dynamic calculation of sustainable retirement spending.

### **3. Professional Asset Management**
- **Granular Tax-Lot Tracking** - Track cost basis and purchase dates for precise tax-loss harvesting analysis.
- **Equity Intelligence** - Real-time unrealized G/L and holding period (LTCG vs STCG) analysis.
- **Real Estate Modeler** - Institutional-grade yield modeling with property tax and management fee tracking.

## 🏃‍♂️ **Quick Start**

### **1. Install Dependencies**
```bash
npm install
```

### **2. Configure AI**
Get a Gemini API key from [Google AI Studio](https://makersuite.google.com/app/apikey) and add it to your `.env` file or enter it directly in the app's AI Settings.

```bash
VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
```

### **3. Run Development Server**
```bash
npm run dev
```

### **4. Run Tests**
```bash
npm run test:run
```
